About me

I just love making things.

Hi, I’m the person behind Heartible. My head is always full of little ideas — a penguin who slides across the ice with his best friend, a battery that prints a receipt for your day, a tiny world you can build piece by piece. Most of them start as a doodle or a “what if…?” and I can’t rest until I’ve made them real.

So I make them with whatever tool fits: code for the apps and games, a laser for keepsakes you can hold, and a 3D printer for the little worlds. Different materials, same feeling — soft, rounded, a little bit playful, and made to make someone smile.

Heartible is a one-person studio in Illinois, and this is where all those ideas land. Thanks for stopping by — I hope something here feels like a small, happy place.
